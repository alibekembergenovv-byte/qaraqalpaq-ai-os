## 2026-09-23 - Prisma Connection Exhaustion and N+1 Queries
**Learning:** The application was establishing new Prisma client instances in individual service files (`new PrismaClient()`) instead of using the Next.js global singleton from `src/lib/db.ts`, risking connection exhaustion. Furthermore, the news scraping processes (`src/app/api/news/scrape/route.ts` and `src/lib/services/news-collector.ts`) used individual `findUnique` and `create` operations within loops, resulting in severe N+1 query bottlenecks and slow insertions.
**Action:** Always import `prisma` from `@/lib/db` across the application. When inserting batches of items, use Prisma's `createMany` with `skipDuplicates: true` and a `Map` or similar strategy to ensure application-level uniqueness before attempting the insertion.
